## Clean City projects

Besides regular cleanups, volunteers can join extra program projects:

- **Eco course with cartoons** for kids and new volunteers.
- **Ekomapa PRO** — map points, routes, community cleanups and moderation.
- **Greening** — planting and plant care with the curator.
- **Recycling** — sorting and dropping off recyclables.
- **Subbotniks** — group cleanups that also count toward the weekly report.

Extra tasks appear in the program chat. Send ideas to curator Anton @Giktom.
