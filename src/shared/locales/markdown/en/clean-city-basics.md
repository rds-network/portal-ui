## Who the volunteer is

**Clean City** is public-space cleanups and eco events in Serbia, under the [Law on Volunteering](https://russian.rs/laws/zakon-o-volonterstve).

## Safety

- Wear gloves and closed shoes.
- Be careful with glass, rusted metal and hazardous items.
- Do not collect medical waste or dead birds.
- Stay safe near roads and water.
- Drink water and take breaks in the heat.

## Trash

- Use strong bags and tie them.
- Leave bags where municipal services collect them.
- Sort plastic, glass and paper when possible.

## Weekly norm — 10 hours

At least **10 hours** per week. Extra materials once every two weeks can count as 1 hour. Curator meetings also go into the report.

## 6 steps

1. Pick a public place. Do not clean commercial lots or fenced sites.
2. Mark the area — you will need a map screenshot.
3. Estimate volume and type of waste.
4. Large dumps you cannot clear: [monitoring form](https://forms.gle/Qa8nxn4KGCTd8WKh7).
5. Photos **before and after** from the same angle, plus bags.
6. Submit a weekly report on the portal.
